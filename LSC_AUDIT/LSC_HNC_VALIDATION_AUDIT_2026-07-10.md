## 6. ACTIONABLE RECOMMENDATIONS

### Recommendation 1: Restructure Claim Hierarchy

**Action:** Position LSC Theory as a **constrained phenomenological framework**, not as confirmed physics. Adopt the same claim-boundary discipline used in the LSC 6.3.0 neutrino validation protocol (which the Prime Sentinel has already approved for physics).

**Allowed claims:**
- LSC is an unvalidated phenomenological framework for symbolic-harmonic interaction.
- The Auris alphabet is a structured encoding protocol with internal consistency.
- The extraction grid model is a useful sociophysical analogy with documented institutional coordination.
- Preliminary acoustic frequency effects (432 vs 440 Hz, 528 Hz) have limited but promising evidence.

**Not allowed (until tested):**
- LSC proves that symbols carry physical frequencies.
- LSC proves that language independently alters electromagnetic fields.
- LSC proves multiversal navigation.
- LSC excludes conventional explanations (diffusion, placebo, confounders).

**Rationale:** This protects the framework from premature dismissal while maintaining scientific rigor. The HNC framework's strength is its mathematical elegance; its weakness is overclaiming before validation.
