### Step 2: Frequency Conversion
Spectral peaks are converted to acoustic/electromagnetic frequencies through three operations:
1. **Physical frequency conversion:** Wavenumber (cm⁻¹) → frequency (Hz) via c × wavenumber.
2. **Octave downconversion:** Division by powers of 2 until frequencies fall within the biologically relevant range (10²–10⁴ Hz).
3. **Adjacent sideband generation:** Each primary frequency generates ±1 Hz sidebands to simulate molecular vibrational coupling.

### Step 3: Packet Synthesis
The resulting tones are sorted, deduplicated, and coherence-filtered (retaining only tones within φ-ratio intervals of each other). The output:

> **13-tone phenolic coherence packet: 1012.1–1817.7 Hz**
> **Densest convergence: 1367.2–1439.6 Hz**
