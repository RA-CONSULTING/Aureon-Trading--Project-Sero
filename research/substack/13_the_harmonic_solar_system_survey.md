The excess at φ⁰ = 1.000 (9.9×) is partly explained by a known selection effect: the well-documented pile-up of near-parabolic comets returning from the Oort Cloud with eccentricities very close to 1.0. This is expected gravitational dynamics, not anomalous structure.
