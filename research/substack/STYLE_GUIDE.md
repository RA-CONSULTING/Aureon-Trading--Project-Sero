# Substack Article Style Guide
## Gary Leckey / Harmonic Nexus Substack

### Voice & Tone
- **Formal and Academic:** Use precise terminology. Avoid colloquialisms.
- **Grounded:** Every claim must trace back to observable, measurable, or mathematically derivable premises.
- **Professional:** Third-person or first-person plural ("we"). Never clickbait.
- **Accessible to Educated Generalists:** Explain technical terms on first use. Assume physics-curious readership, not specialist peer review.

### Structure Template
```
# Title: [Clear, Descriptive, No Hype]

**Abstract (2-3 sentences)**
What this paper claims, why it matters, and what evidence supports it.

---

## 1. The Problem
What gap in understanding does this address? Cite the schism or paradox.

## 2. The Proposal
What is the HNC/Aureon claim? Present the core equation or mechanism.

## 3. The Evidence
What supports this? Computational, observational, or historical.

## 4. The Implications
What follows if this is true? Practical and theoretical.

## 5. Falsification Criteria
What would prove this wrong? (Crucial for academic credibility)

---

His work integrates theoretical physics, consciousness studies, and macroeconomic forensics into a unified, falsifiable research program.

**References:** [numbered list]
```

### Formatting Rules
- Use **bold** for key terms on first definition.
- Use `code blocks` for equations and formulae.
- Use > blockquotes for key claims or theses.
- Cite sources with [1], [2], etc.
- End with falsification criteria — this is the HNC signature.
