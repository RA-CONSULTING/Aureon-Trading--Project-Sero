Not all capital is equal. Some investors extract more value than they create. The Aureon framework detects these patterns.

### EXTRACTION INDICATORS (Red Flags)

| Indicator | What It Means | Which Investors |
|-----------|--------------|-----------------|
| Board control at pre-seed | Founder loses decision-making power before product-market fit | BGF, Foresight, some Delta deals |
| Liquidation preference >1x | Investor gets paid first in any exit, even at a loss | Growth-stage VCs generally |

### The EII Tax Relief Angle (Republic of Ireland)

For ROI investors: Aureon can qualify for the Employment Investment Incentive (EII) scheme, offering 40% tax relief to investors. This is a **financial incentive** that reduces the investor's net cost by 40% while Aureon receives full value. This is a harmonic amplification — the investor wins, the company wins, the state wins.

**Critical Note:** EII requires Irish Ltd company status. R&A Consulting and Brokerage Services Ltd (NI696693) is a Northern Ireland company. EII may apply cross-border but requires verification. This is a question to ask Enterprise Ireland / InterTradeIreland.
